Before you start

  • Check the site. The official web address is announced at launch. Type it yourself or use a bookmark, and check the address bar before you connect a wallet.
  • Use a wallet that works on Kaia. Kaia Wallet, MetaMask with the Kaia network added (chain ID 8217), or any wallet that connects through WalletConnect.

Step by step

  1. Connect

    Open the Swap page, choose Connect wallet and pick your wallet. If it is on another network, the page offers one button to switch to Kaia.

  2. Approve

    The first time, your wallet asks you to let the swap contract move the old CT you chose. This is its own transaction and costs a little gas.

  3. Swap

  4. Wait 7 days

  6. Add new CT to your wallet

    Choose Add new CT to wallet, or add the token by hand with the new CT address under Contracts and audit. Compare the full address, not the ticker.

Special cases

CT on an exchange
The exchange handles the swap for CT it holds for you. The list of exchanges is to be announced. Follow your exchange's own notice.
CT in a Klayswap pool
Remove your liquidity first so the CT is back in your wallet, then swap it.
Smart contract wallets and multisigs
Lost access
If you lost the seed phrase or private key for a wallet, no one can move its CT, including Cojam. The team cannot swap on your behalf.
Very small balances

Safety

  • Never share your seed phrase or private key. No real page or person needs it.
  • No one from Cojam will message you first. Anyone who contacts you about the swap in a DM is not us.
  • Use only official links. Ignore links in replies, ads and DMs.
  • A swap never asks you to send CT to a person or a wallet. You only interact with the swap contract.
  • Check full addresses. The ticker CT is used by other tokens too, including some listed on MEXC. Compare the full contract address before you add, buy or swap a token.

Contracts and audit

  • New CT is a plain KIP-7 token (ERC-20 compatible, 18 decimals) with no pause, no mint, no blacklist and no owner powers.
  • Audit in progress The report will be linked here when it is published.
