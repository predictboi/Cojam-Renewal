Before you start

  • Check the site. The only official site is cojam.app. Type it yourself or use a bookmark, and check the address bar before you connect a wallet.
  • Use a wallet that works on Kaia. Kaia Wallet, MetaMask with the Kaia network added (chain ID 8217), or any wallet that connects through WalletConnect.

Which wallet should I use?

The connect sheet offers three ways in. The swap is the same in each; only the network fee differs.

  • Kaia Wallet Free, recommended

    Cojam pays the network fee for approving, swapping and claiming, so you need no KAIA. On a phone, choose Open in Kaia Wallet.

  • MetaMask Small KAIA fee

    You pay a small network fee in KAIA for each approval, swap and claim. Keep a little KAIA in the same wallet. On a phone, choose Open in MetaMask.

  • WalletConnect Small KAIA fee for now

    Connects other wallet apps. For now you pay a small network fee in KAIA for each step, as with MetaMask.

Step by step

  1. Connect

    Open the Swap page, choose Connect wallet and pick your wallet. If it is on another network, the page offers one button to switch to Kaia.

  2. Approve

    The first time, your wallet asks you to let the swap contract move the old CT you chose. It is its own transaction: free with Kaia Wallet; a small KAIA fee with MetaMask.

  3. Swap

    Enter an amount (your full balance is filled in) and confirm. Your old CT goes to the swap contract, and in the same transaction 10% of the new CT arrives in your wallet. The other 90% is locked for 7 days.

  4. Wait 7 days

    The Swap page shows the date and a countdown for the 90%. You can close the page; nothing else is needed while you wait.

  5. Receive the 90%

    After 7 days a Cojam keeper sends the 90% to your wallet automatically, shortly after the unlock, and pays the gas. You do not need to come back. If you do not want to wait, open the Swap page with the same wallet and choose Claim.

  6. Add new CT to your wallet

    Choose Add new CT to wallet, or add the token by hand with the new CT address under Contracts and audit. Compare the full address, not the ticker.

Lock rules

  • Each swap is its own lot. 10% of it arrives at once, and the other 90% has its own 7 day lock. Swap twice and you get two lots with two unlock dates.
  • The 10% is rounded down to the smallest unit of CT and the locked part is the rest, so the two always add up to exactly what you swapped.
  • The lock starts when the swap is confirmed on chain and ends exactly 7 days later.
  • After the unlock, anyone can trigger the release, but it always pays the wallet that swapped. Cojam runs a keeper that sends it shortly after the unlock and pays the gas. No one can redirect it.
  • You can also claim it yourself at any time after the unlock, for example if the keeper is late. There is no deadline.
  • Why the lock: it deters swap abuse and favours real holders. Only 10% is free at once, so swapping just to sell straight away does not pay.
  • The swap reserve sits in the swap contract itself, so anyone can check it on chain at any time. The new CT owed to locked 90% remainders is set aside in the contract, and no one, not even Cojam, can move it.

Special cases

  • CT on an exchange

    The exchange handles the swap for CT it holds for you. The list of exchanges is to be announced. Follow your exchange's own notice.

  • CT in a Klayswap pool

    Remove your liquidity first so the CT is back in your wallet, then swap it.

  • Smart contract wallets and multisigs

    Any Kaia wallet that can approve and call the swap contract can swap. A multisig needs its signers for the approval and the swap. The 90% is released to the same address that swapped.

  • Lost access

    If you lost the seed phrase or private key for a wallet, no one can move its CT, including Cojam. The team cannot swap on your behalf.

  • Very small balances

    Any amount above 0 can be swapped. With MetaMask, the gas in KAIA may cost more than a very small amount of CT is worth; with Kaia Wallet the swap is free.

Fees

The swap charges no fee and the rate is always 1 old CT to 1 new CT. The only cost is the Kaia network fee, and with Kaia Wallet Cojam pays it for you.

  • Kaia Wallet: free. Kaia supports fee delegation: you sign the transaction in your wallet, and Cojam's fee payer co-signs it and pays the fee. You need no KAIA to approve, swap or claim. Over WalletConnect, plan on a small KAIA fee for now.
  • MetaMask: a small fee in KAIA. MetaMask cannot sign Kaia fee delegated transactions, so you pay the network fee yourself for each approval, swap and claim. Your wallet shows the exact fee before you confirm.
  • What the fee payer can do. It only pays fees for approving old CT to the swap contract, swapping and claiming on the Cojam contracts, with a limit per wallet. It cannot sign for you and can never move your tokens.
  • If the fee payer is busy. The Swap page says so and nothing is sent. Retry, or pay the fee yourself in KAIA.
  • The automatic release is always free. The keeper pays the gas to send you the 90%, from the same Cojam gas wallet.

Get KAIA for MetaMask

Only MetaMask users need KAIA. With Kaia Wallet the swap is free, so the simplest fix is to swap with Kaia Wallet instead.

  • Buy KAIA on an exchange that lists it, then withdraw it to the same wallet address that holds your old CT.
  • Pick the Kaia network when you withdraw. KAIA sent on another network does not arrive in your Kaia wallet.
  • How much: Kaia fees are small, and well under 1 KAIA covers approving, swapping and claiming. Your wallet shows the exact fee before you confirm.

Safety

  • Never share your seed phrase or private key. No real page or person needs it.
  • No one from Cojam will message you first. Anyone who contacts you about the swap in a DM is not us.
  • Use only official links. Ignore links in replies, ads and DMs.
  • A swap never asks you to send CT to a person or a wallet. You only interact with the swap contract.
  • Check full addresses. The ticker CT is used by other tokens too, including some listed on MEXC. Compare the full contract address before you add, buy or swap a token.

Contracts and audit

  • New CT is a plain KIP-7 token (ERC-20 compatible, 18 decimals) with no owner, no mint, no pause and no blacklist. The swap contract has no owner and no pause either, and no one can change the 1:1 rate, the 10%, the 7 day lock, the window or any swap already made. Its reserve is visible on chain, and the new CT owed to locked 90% is set aside so no one can move it. Old CT received by the swap contract stays there forever.
