Timeline

One year
to swap

Swaps are open for 1 year from launch. Locked remainders still release after that.

  1. Window opens

    On launch, the day the swap contract is deployed. Open to every holder alike.

  2. On every swap

    10% now, 90% 7 days later

    10% arrives in the swap itself. The 90% is sent to your wallet automatically 7 days later.

  3. Window closes

    1 year after launch. No new swaps after this.

  4. After the window

    Remainders still release

    The locked 90% of earlier swaps is still sent to its owners.

Stay safe

Three rules

  1. Never share your seed phrase. No one from Cojam will ask for it or message you first.

  3. Swap only on cojam.app. A swap never asks you to send CT to anyone.
