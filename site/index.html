How the swap works

You sign twice in your own wallet, to approve and to swap. The rest happens on its own.

  1. Swap old CT

    Approve, then swap any amount, one for one.

  2. 10% arrives now

    In the same transaction, 10% of your new CT lands in your wallet.

  3. 90% after 7 days

    The other 90% is locked for 7 days, one tick per day.

  4. Sent to your wallet automatically

    Cojam sends the 90% to your wallet and pays the gas. You can also claim it yourself.

No KAIA needed with Kaia Wallet: Cojam pays the network fee.

One year to swap

Swaps are open for 1 year from launch. Locked remainders still release after that.

  1. Window opens

    On launch, the day the swap contract is deployed. Open to every holder.

  2. On every swap

    10% now, 90% 7 days later

    10% arrives in the swap itself. The 90% is sent to your wallet automatically 7 days later.

  3. Window closes

    1 year after launch. No new swaps. Unswapped new CT goes to the new treasury multisig.

  4. After the window

    Remainders still release

    The locked 90% of earlier swaps is still sent to its owners.
