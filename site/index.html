How the swap works

Three steps, all signed in your own wallet.

  1. Swap old CT

    Approve the swap contract, then swap any amount.

  2. 7 day lock

    Your new CT is held for 7 days, one tick per day.

  3. Claim new CT

    After the lock ends, claim it to your wallet. Each step costs a little gas.

One year to swap

The window is open for 1 year. Claims never close.

  1. Window opens

    Swaps start for every holder.

  2. 7 days after each swap

    Each swap unlocks

    Claim it any time after that.

  3. Window closes

    No new swaps after this date. New CT that was not swapped returns to the Cojam treasury wallet.

  4. After the window

    Claims stay open

    Swaps already locked can still be claimed.

Questions

More detail in the swap docs

My CT is on an exchange. What do I do?

Nothing yet. Supporting exchanges will swap it for you. The list will be announced.

My CT is in a Klayswap pool.

Remove your liquidity first so the CT is back in your wallet, then swap it.

What if I miss the window?

After the window closes, new swaps are not possible. Swaps you already made can still be claimed at any time.

Is there a reward for swapping?

No. It is one for one: 1 old CT gives 1 new CT, with no bonus and no penalty.

Why the 7 day lock?

It spreads new CT out over time instead of releasing it all at once, and gives time to catch mistakes or abuse before tokens move.

Which wallets work?

Kaia Wallet, MetaMask with the Kaia network added, and wallets that connect through WalletConnect.

What does it cost?

Only Kaia network gas, paid in KAIA, for the approval, the swap and the claim. Cojam charges no fee.

Is it safe?

You keep custody the whole time and sign every step yourself. Never share your seed phrase: no one from Cojam will ever ask for it.
